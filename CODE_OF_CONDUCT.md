# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Anyone who takes part in ci-parity, by opening an issue, sending a pull
request, reviewing a change or asking a question, should be treated with
respect, whatever their background or experience.

## Our standards

**Expected behavior:**

- Being kind, patient and professional
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Respecting other people's privacy

**Unacceptable behavior:**

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or imagery
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies to the repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and
commits, and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to <hello@jimmykurian.me>. Reports are kept
confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and
pull requests that break this code, and may block anyone who repeatedly or
seriously breaks it.
